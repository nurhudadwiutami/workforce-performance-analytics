# Workforce Performance & Operational Capacity Analytics (Strategic OD)

## Executive Summary
This project evaluates workforce performance, capacity bottlenecks, and training efficiency across organizational departments. By integrating performance rating compositions, employee Work-Life Balance (WLB) scores, and training budget utilization datasets, the analysis identified critical operational risks, severe training budget leakages, and workload-driven burnout.

The objective is to provide data-backed Strategic OD recommendations to optimize capacity allocation, mitigate burnout, and eliminate training waste.

---

## Key Insights & Risks

### 1. Performance Rating Composition & Risk Profiles
* **High Operational Volume (Production):** Production accounts for **69.7% of all PIP cases** across the firm (60 out of 86 employees), demanding the largest allocation of HR/OD counseling resources.
* **Critical Proportional Risk (Executive Office):** Executive Office exhibits a **21% PIP rate**, signaling an urgent need for executive alignment.
* **Technical Watchlist (IT/IS):** IT/IS records 16 PIP cases and 19 Needs Improvement instances, making it a key priority for targeted technical upskilling.

### 2. Training Effectiveness & $776K Budget Leakage
* **Total Budget Leakage:** The company lost **$776K (48.8%)** of its total $1.59M training budget due to failed or incomplete programs.
* **Technical Skills Deficit:** Technical Skills suffered the highest waste ratio at **55.4% ($168K waste)**, directly mirroring the high rate of PIP/Needs Improvement cases in IT/IS and Production.
* **Leadership Alignment Gap:** Leadership Development wasted **46.9% ($143K)** of its budget, corroborating the elevated performance risk in the Executive Office.

### 3. Workload & Burnout Assessment (Performance vs. Capacity Disconnect)
* **Executive Leadership Disconnect:** Executive Office achieves the highest Work-Life Balance score (**3.29**) yet yields the lowest average performance rating (**2.79**), proving a systemic management alignment issue rather than overwork.
* **Operational Capacity Bottlenecks:** Work-Life Balance drops below target in Production (**2.97**) and IT/IS (**2.98**). Paired with the 55.4% technical training failure rate, low performance stems from operational burnout and overcapacity.

## Strategic Action Plan & Business Solutions

1. **Leadership Alignment & Executive Accountability:**
   * Address Executive Office performance (2.79 rating, 46.9% training waste) by replacing generic off-site workshops with KPI-driven Executive Coaching tied directly to divisional targets.
   * Enforce mandatory **90-day post-training execution evaluations**.

2. **Technical Capacity & Workload Rebalancing:**
   * Solve burnout in IT/IS and Production (WLB < 2.98, 55.4% technical training failure rate) by conducting a comprehensive **Workforce Capacity Audit**.
   * Restructure technical training into modular, **micro-learning sessions** integrated into regular shifts to prevent further capacity strain.

3. **Process Restructuring & Administrative Optimization:**
   * Address Admin Offices satisfaction drop (2.51 score) by conducting a targeted **Process Friction Review**.
   * Eliminate repetitive bottlenecks, refine role clarity, and modernize daily operational tools to restore employee engagement.
